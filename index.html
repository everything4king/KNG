03 / THE FOUNDER

THE VISION BEHIND THE CROWN

EMMANUEL
ISAAC O.

CEO & FOUNDER, KNG.

“I didn't start KNG just to create clothes. I started it to create a standard.”

KNG was born from a desire to build something that represents more than fashion — a brand that gives people the confidence to embrace who they are, create their own lane, and never be afraid to stand apart.

I wanted to create something that reflects the mindset I believe in: confidence, ambition, discipline and individuality.

The crown became the symbol of that vision. Not a symbol of being above anyone, but a reminder to know your worth, believe in yourself, and have the courage to become the person you were meant to be.

KNG is my vision brought to life — and this is only the beginning.
